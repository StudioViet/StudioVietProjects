## Status definitions

- **Completed professional work:** Delivered within a prior employment context; public details are sanitized.
- **Operating community:** Active real-world initiative with verified participation and operating evidence.
- **Pilot / Review:** Working system or workflow under validation; not represented as a finished commercial product.
- **In Progress:** Active build with known gaps and no implied completion.
- **Prototype research:** Tested concept with documented discovery and iteration, not a marketed finished product.
