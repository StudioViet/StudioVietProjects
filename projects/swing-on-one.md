# Swing on One

**Status:** Operating community

## Problem

Asian American and other Minnesota volleyball players lacked a consistent, affordable community where they could find games, build relationships, and participate without already knowing the right people.

## What I did

- Started the community with approximately five people
- Recruited players through open gyms and local networks
- Coordinated with schools, coaches, and recreation partners for gym access
- Built a seven-person volunteer administration team
- Established repeatable communication and event-support practices

## Evidence

- Approximately 350 members
- More than 100 organized events
- Games held multiple times per week during active seasons
- Community relationships that extended into leagues, tournaments, and personal milestones

## What this demonstrates

Community building, stakeholder coordination, volunteer leadership, operating-system design, inclusive program development, and sustained execution.

## Operating principle

Community should not take years to find. The system is designed to reduce the friction between wanting to participate and feeling that you belong.
