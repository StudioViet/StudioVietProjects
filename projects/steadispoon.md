# SteadiSpoon

**Status:** Prototype research

## Problem

People living with hand tremors can face difficulty with everyday eating tasks. SteadiSpoon explored a stabilizing utensil concept through customer discovery and iterative product design.

## Work completed

- Analyzed feedback from 60 people with Parkinson's disease
- Developed and evaluated more than 10 feature iterations
- Used structured product discovery and Agile/Scrum practices to prioritize changes
- Documented a 32% reduction in measured tremor motion during prototype work

## My role

Product adviser and researcher connecting user needs, engineering constraints, test evidence, and feature decisions.

## What this demonstrates

Biomedical product thinking, customer discovery, requirements prioritization, experimentation, evidence-based iteration, and cross-functional communication.

## Known limitations

This is prototype evidence, not a claim of clinical efficacy, regulatory clearance, or a commercial medical product.
